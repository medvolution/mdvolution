    What is Medvolution?

    We are a group of Occupational Health (OH) specialists who, through a collaborative initiative, drive continuous improvement in this field through a multidisciplinary approach.

    Disciplines

    Physicians, nurses, engineers, chemists, psychologists, human resources professionals, entrepreneurs and paramedics are some of the professions that take part in our project.

  • Occupational Legion

    The Occupational Legion is a program made up of Occupational Health experts who volunteer their time at Medvolution.

    The initiative brings together specialists who answer questions, analyze regulations and share knowledge with professionals at every level, from beginners to established experts.

    The program is completely free and carries no obligations. It operates on a volunteer basis, according to the availability of its members.

    Our main channel of communication is social media, including WhatsApp, Telegram and Facebook groups.

    Would you like to share your experience with other Occupational Health professionals? Visit our contact section for more information.
