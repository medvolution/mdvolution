A Medvolution initiative to promote the use of health technologies, especially in Occupational Health.

What is HYDRA

HYDRA is the Medvolution division dedicated to advancing software, wearables and advanced surveillance and risk analysis systems, grounded in data and research.

Pillars

  • Health technologY

    Health technologies applied to Occupational Health.

  • Data

    Data to understand risks and make better decisions.

  • Research

    Applied research and collaboration with professionals.

  • Analytics

    Analysis to anticipate and manage occupational risks.

Areas of work

  • Software

    Digital tools for managing Occupational Health.

  • Wearables

    Devices for monitoring working conditions.

  • Advanced surveillance

    Systems for tracking and alerting on risks.

  • Risk analysis

    Assessment and prioritization of occupational risks.

Featured project: ORION

Occupational Risk Intelligence & Operational Navigation (ORION), version V1.0.1.
