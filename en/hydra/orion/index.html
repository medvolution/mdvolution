ORION stands for Occupational Risk Intelligence & Operational Navigation.

A web platform for preventive epidemiological risk intelligence and operational continuity support.

What ORION means

Occupational Risk Intelligence & Operational Navigation

  • Occupational

    Relating to occupational health.

  • Risk

    The likelihood and impact of a hazard.

  • Intelligence

    Structured analysis of public data.

  • Operational

    Relating to day-to-day operations.

  • Navigation

    Guidance for decisions under uncertainty.

What ORION is

ORION brings together public sources to estimate the preventive epidemiological risk of countries and territories, and presents it in clear dashboards to support travel and operational continuity decisions.

What it does

  • Global risk

    Epidemiological risk by country.

  • Mexico risk

    Epidemiological risk by state, based on official data.

  • Travel alerts

    Travel alerts from official sources.

  • Preventive score

    A 1 to 100 scale with risk levels.

  • Secure dashboards

    Protected visualisation with controlled access.

  • Reports

    Public preview. Full reports for authorised members only.

Notice

ORION is a preventive decision-support tool based on mathematical models and public sources. It does not replace official sources or expert judgement.
